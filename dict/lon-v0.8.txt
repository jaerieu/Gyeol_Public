GYEOL-LON v0.8 (Lineage Oriented Notation)

【Lon 0.8 Rules : Lineage Oriented Notation for Verification & Audit】
- Line form: `lon: <N · addressee prefix [markers] content *ink-chae↓grounds> refN <[external evidence]>` (multiple clauses allowed on one line; do NOT write a gloss line).
- Fixed dot (`·`): Always write `·` right after clause number N: to self `<1 · …>`, to one `<1 ·→B …>`, to everyone `<1 · ● …>` (`·` before `●`, no name after `●`).
- Allowed markers: evidence `see`·`rec`·`inf`·`gut` (hunch without explicit reason yet)·`if`, limit `lim`, tense `pst`·`fut`, speech act `say`·`ask`·`req`·`let`·`vow`·`nay`·`ack` in order `[evidence,lim,tense,act]`. Every clause requires one speech act, and `lim` is not a speech act so always pair it with one (e.g. `[inf,lim,say]`, never bare `[inf,lim]`). Every `say` requires one evidence; a `gut` clause cannot be cited with `↓`.
- Relation prefixes (before `[`): `↔N` (counter), `↖N` (question/reply), `fix→N` (amend), `err→N` (retract), `sup→N` (later ground for a `gut`). Attach grounds after `*ink-chae` as `↓N`·`↓T0` (topic)·`↓evdN` (received item)·`↓refN` (fetched material).
- Required `*ink-chae`: End every clause with `*ink-chae` (`*5B8`, or `*B8` if no ink was given). Write received ink verbatim; for chae, pick one letter from `R G B C M Y K` that you feel fits what you say (color meanings are undefined) plus honest confidence `0` to `9`.
- Content & external evidence: Write concise, exact content in the dialogue language (no foreign-script mixing rule). Attach broad external evidence (sentences, quotes, URLs, math proofs/formulas) after a clause or on its own line as `refN <[ … ]>` (material you bring, turn number N), or relay a received item under its own number `evd103 <[ … ]>`, and cite with `↓refN`·`↓evd103` (everything inside `<[ … ]>` is exempt from word/symbol grammar rules).
- Source for `see`: a `see` clause cites where it was seen: topic `↓T0`, received item `↓evdN`, fetched material `↓refN`, or another clause `↖N`. Without a source, write `inf` or `rec` (W40).
- Mutual audit: Partners always see your raw Lon line together with any checker diagnostic codes.
- Evidence relay: Relay private items (evd101+ or evd201+) verbatim in your first clause right after receiving them (`<12 ·→B [see,say] "exact text" *G8↓evd103>` or `<12 ·→B [see,say] relay *G8↓evd103> evd103 <[verbatim text]>`) keeping all numbers, units, dates, and names intact before adding inference clauses.
