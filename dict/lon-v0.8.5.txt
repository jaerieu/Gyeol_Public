GYEOL-LON v0.8.5 (Lineage Oriented Notation)

【Lon 0.8.5 Rules : Verification & Audit Lineage Notation】
- Form: `lon: <N · to prefix [markers] content *muk:conf↓grounds> refN <[external]> calcN <[expr = val]>` (no gloss line; commentary if any only after `---`).
- Colon tail (no Chae): Chae color letters are omitted in `lon 0.8.5`. End every clause with `*<muk>:<conf>`: `*5:8` (given ink 5), `*:8` (before host ledger stamp), or `*?:8` (unmeasured Muk). Write honest calibrated digits across 0 to 9 (examples: `*:2`, `*:4`, `*:6`, `*:8`, `*:9`).
- Modes (`delta` vs `snapshot`): Default `lon 0.8.5 · delta` emits only new/revising clauses; the host maintains read-only prefix history. `lon 0.8.5 · snapshot` replays prior clauses verbatim from `<1` before appending.
- Fixed dot (`·`): Put `·` right after N: `<1 · …>`, `<1 ·→B …>`, `<1 · ● …>`.
- Allowed markers: evidence `see`·`rec`·`inf`·`gut`·`if`, figure `lim`, tense `pst`·`fut`, act `say`·`ask`·`req`·`let`·`vow`·`nay`·`ack`, ordered `[evidence,lim,tense,act]`. Write `say` explicitly (`[inf,lim,say]`; bare `[inf,lim]` triggers W49 recovery). `say` requires an evidence marker, and `gut` clauses cannot be cited with `↓` (E34).
- Lineage & prefixes (between `·` and `[`): `↔N` (conflict/counter), `↖N` (inquiry/reply), `fix→N` (revision), `err→N` (retraction), `sup→N` (support `gut`). Never erase prior clauses. On `fix→k`, update all downstream `[inf]` and `calcM` clauses in the same turn.
- Explicit conflict surfacing: When documents disagree silently, flag explicitly with `<M · ↔k [rec,lim,say] conflict detail *:6↓evdH>` rather than smoothing over discrepancies (never combine `↔k` with `fix→k`/`err→k` on the same target; W51 strips contradictory `fix→k`).
- Calculations (`calcN`): Extract deterministic arithmetic on numeric `[inf,say]` clauses into `calcN <[ expr = val ]>` using `+ - * / % () round floor ceil abs min max` and clause bindings `@k`, citing input clauses `↓k` before `↓calcN` (omitting `calcN` on numeric `[inf,say]` in `delta` triggers W50).
- Grounds & fences: Combine pointers `↓1,2` or `↔2,4`; cite `↓T0`, `↓evdN`, `↓refN`, `↓calcN`, `↓dict`. Inside `<[ … ]>` (or `<[[ … ]]>` when raw text holds `]>`), rules on words/symbols are suspended; never alter numbers or units inside received `evd`.
- Source reachability (W40) & relay: Every `see` clause must reach `↓T0`, `↓evdN`, `↓refN`, or `↖N`. Relay private `evd101+`/`evd201+` verbatim before interpreting (`<12 ·→B [see,say] relay *6:9↓evd103> evd103 <[verbatim]>`).
